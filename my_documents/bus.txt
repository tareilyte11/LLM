EU rules on bus and coach rights apply if your journey starts or finishes in an EU country , and mainly apply to regular long-distance bus and coach services. These rights apply if you are travelling with regular services that operate along specified routes where passengers are picked up and set down at predefined stopping points according to a set timetable. Long-distance means that the scheduled distance of the service, not your individual trip, is 250 km or more.

Throughout your journey, the bus or coach carrier must provide clear and correct information about the service and your passenger rights. This information should also be made available in a format accessible for people with disabilities .

Your bus or coach services was cancelled; Your bus or coach service was delayed at departure; Your bus or coach service left before the scheduled departure time; Your luggage was lost or damaged in an accident; You were injured in a bus or coach accident; You are a dependant of a victim of a fatal bus or coach accident; Your rights as a passenger with disabilities or reduced mobility were not respected.

If you think your rights have not been respected, you should make a complaint to the bus or coach company within 3 months of the scheduled service during which the problem occurred. The bus or coach company must react within 1 month and give you a final reply 3 months after receiving your complaint. If you are not satisfied with the reply, you can lodge a complaint with the relevant national authority in your country. The national authority should provide you with a non-binding legal opinion on how to proceed with your claim.

You can also try to resolve your dispute using out-of-court procedures or an alternative dispute resolution entity . Alternative dispute resolution is only available to EU residents.

You may wish to resort to formal legal action and submit a claim for compensation under EU rules using the European Small Claims procedure . You may also bring the matter before the courts in the country where the bus or coach company is registered. The time limits for bringing an action against a bus or coach company in a national court are defined in accordance with national rules on the limitation of actions in each EU country.

You can also consult your local European Consumer Centre for help and advice on problems related to bus or coach passenger rights.

If the scheduled bus or coach service you're booked on is cancelled, the bus / coach carrier or the terminal managing body must inform you of the situation as soon as possible - and no later than 30 minutes after the scheduled departure time.

If your long distance (250 km or more) coach or bus service is cancelled due to overbooking, an early departure or for operational reasons, the company must offer you a choice between: the reimbursement of your ticket within 14 days and, where necessary, a free return service to the initial departure point of the service at the earliest opportunity (if the delay prevents you from fulfilling the purpose of your trip) or; continuing your journey or re-routing to your final destination under comparable conditions at no extra cost at the earliest opportunity.

If your long-distance (250 km or more) coach or bus service is cancelled and the duration of the journey was scheduled to last more than 3 hours, you are also entitled to: snacks, meals or refreshments, in proportion to the waiting time, provided they are available or can reasonably be supplied on the bus or in the terminal; accommodation if you have to stay overnight – for up to 2 nights, at a maximum rate of €80 per night – and transport to your accommodation and return to the terminal.

If your bus or coach service was cancelled due to severe weather conditions or a natural disaster endangering the safe operation of the bus or coach, the carrier does not have to cover accommodation expenses or transport costs to your accommodation.

If the scheduled bus or coach service you're booked on is delayed at departure (for road safety reasons), the bus / coach carrier or the terminal managing body must inform you: of the situation as soon as possible - and no later than 30 minutes after the scheduled departure time, and; of the estimated departure time as soon as this information is available.

If your long-distance (250 km or more) coach or bus service is delayed at departure for more than 2 hours, the bus company must offer you a choice between: the reimbursement of your ticket and, where necessary, a free return service to the initial departure point of the service at the earliest opportunity (if the delay prevents you from fulfilling the purpose of your trip), or; continuing your journey or re-routing under comparable conditions, to your final destination at the earliest opportunity at no extra cost.

If the carrier of the service does not offer you these options when the delay occurs, you can submit a complaint at a later stage, and claim a refund of your ticket as well as compensation equal to 50% of the ticket price. The carrier must pay this compensation within 1 month of your request.

If your long-distance (250 km or more) coach or bus service was scheduled to last more than 3 hours and the departure is delayed by more than 90 minutes, in addition to the above, you are also entitled to: snacks, meals or refreshments in proportion to the waiting time, provided they are available or can reasonably be supplied on the bus or in the terminal; accommodation if you have to stay overnight – for up to 2 nights, at a maximum rate of €80 per night – and transport to your accommodation and return to the terminal.

If the delay was caused by severe weather conditions or a natural disaster, the carrier does not have to cover accommodation costs.

If your bus or coach left before the scheduled departure time, it is considered as a cancellation. In this case, you have the same rights that apply when a bus or coach service is cancelled .

If your luggage or other personal belongings are lost or damaged because of a bus or coach accident on a long-distance journey (250 km or more) you have a right to compensation. The exact amount of compensation is set by national rules.

If your wheelchair or other mobility aid is damaged or lost, you are entitled to compensation to cover the cost of replacing or repairing it.

Your luggage was lost or stolen during your journey EU rules do not apply if your luggage is lost or stolen during your bus or coach journey but you may be able to make a claim under national rules. Contact the bus or coach carrier and check the terms and conditions of your ticket for more information.

If you are injured in a bus or coach accident during a long-distance journey (250 km or more), you are entitled to compensation. The exact amount of compensation is set by national rules.

The bus or coach carrier must also help provide first aid, for example by calling an ambulance, and provide for any other immediate practical needs you may have, including food, clothes and, if necessary, accommodation.

If you are a dependant of a victim of a fatal bus or coach accident, you have the right to compensation, including funeral expenses. The exact amount of compensation is set by national rules.
